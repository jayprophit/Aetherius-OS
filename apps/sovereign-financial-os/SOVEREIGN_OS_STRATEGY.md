# Sovereign Financial OS: Master Strategy & Roadmap

This document outlines your path from £1 micro-investing to a full-scale Family Office, as discussed in our strategy sessions.

## Phase 1: The Engine Room (Months 1-3)
**Goal**: Automate £1/day growth to build seed capital.
- **Action**: Set up a **Pionex DCA Bot** (BTC/USDT).
- **Deposit**: £40 initial (covers first month + buffer).
- **Configuration**: £1.00 daily buy. 
- **Learning**: Spend 15 mins/day on **TradingView Paper Trading** (Risk-Free).

## Phase 2: The Real-World Pivot (Months 4-6)
**Goal**: Diversify into physical assets and tax-free wrappers.
- **Trading 212 ISA**: Buy £10 of **VWRP ETF** (Vanguard All-World).
- **Goldwise**: Buy £5 of physical gold (stored in Swiss vaults).
- **Debt Snowball**: Use any surplus capital to clear high-interest debt first.

## Phase 3: Core Wealth Engines (Months 7-12)
**Goal**: Expand asset classes and maximize government bonuses.
- **Lifetime ISA (LISA)**: Open with **Moneybox/AJ Bell** for a 25% government bonus.
- **Crypto Staking**: Open **Gemini/Bitget** and stake ETH/SOL for passive yield.
- **Tax Sinking Fund**: Start setting aside 20% of profits for future HMRC obligations.

## Phase 4: Advanced Automation (Year 2)
**Goal**: Accumulate BTC and trade global sessions 24/7.
- **Bitsgap LOOP Bot**: Use BTC as base currency to farm more BTC via altcoin pairs.
- **Global Sessions**: Monitor London, New York, Tokyo, and Sydney handover points.
- **Dividend Equities**: Research and add defensive UK stocks (e.g., LGEN) to the ISA.

## Phase 5: Sovereignty & Structure (Year 3+)
**Goal**: Transition to professional structures.
- **Sole Trader**: Register for Self Assessment if side-business income exceeds £1,000.
- **Limited Company (Ltd)**: Incorporate if annual profits exceed £50,000 for tax efficiency.
- **Multi-Sig Security**: Move significant holdings to a 2-of-3 Hardware Wallet setup (Safe/Casa).

1. **Emergency First**: Never touch the £1,000 emergency fund for trading.
