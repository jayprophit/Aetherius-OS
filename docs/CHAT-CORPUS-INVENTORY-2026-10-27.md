## Method and limits

- ChatGPT exports are parsed from `mapping[*].message.content.parts`.
- Claude/DeepSeek exports are parsed from `chat_messages`/`messages` text fields.
- `chat.html` is scanned in 2 MB chunks; its individual threads are not separated.
- Binary files (installers, images, archives) are counted but not read.
- Scores are triage signals. A high score means the thread is worth reading; it
  does not prove a claim in it.
